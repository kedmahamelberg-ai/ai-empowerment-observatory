How coverage and developments are counted

Each AI-related source page dated in the weekly period counts once in the coverage total. Pages about the same concrete occurrence are grouped into one development.

Additional reports = coverage items minus distinct developments. They measure extra attention, not extra reality.

How new and seen-before are decided

A development is new to AIEO when it is not matched to an accepted event in the disclosed historical pool. A later source about an earlier event increases attention but does not create a new development.

Long-gap candidates are compared with the full registry. Human-governed decisions can revise earlier releases, and prior revisions remain archived.

How the secondary human empowerment index works

Expanding effects receive a positive score, contracting effects receive a negative score, and mixed or no-direct-change cases receive zero. Unclear cases are excluded.

The public index runs from -100 to +100. It is accompanied by a plain-language label and the scored versus unclear denominator.

What AIEO does not claim

The five discovery markets are a research sample, not a complete census of global AI news. A search market is not necessarily the location of the reported development.

The relationship lens classifies how source evidence represents the relation. It does not establish objective system performance, private experience, consciousness, intention, or biological fitness.
