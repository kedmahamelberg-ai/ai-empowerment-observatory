The evidence journey

Five steps, one connected chain

Technical detail is available, but the public method can be followed without reading code or model output.

1

Observe

AIEO stores each canonical article once and records every later search observation separately. An old page returning to search is visibility persistence, not a newly published item.

2

Resolve

Articles are matched to concrete real-world events. Same topic, country, organisation or technology is not enough to merge two records.

3

Remember

New candidates are compared with the full disclosed historical event pool. A long delay lowers confidence; it does not stop comparison.

4

Interpret

AIEO codes the human side and the AI or operator side independently, derives the relationship pattern, and keeps human empowerment and narrative tone as secondary lenses.

5

Revise

Later evidence can merge, unlink or connect records. The current series is restated, while the original published revision remains archived.

Plain-language counting

What do the public numbers mean?

The website uses simpler words than the database, while keeping the technical terms available for audit.

Coverage item

An AI-related page returned by AIEO's Google News searches and dated within the weekly period. It may come from a newspaper, broadcaster, specialist publication, government body, university, policy organisation, research outlet or newsletter. It does not mean only an academic journal article.

Development

One real-world occurrence after AIEO groups coverage items that describe the same thing. The technical database term is event record.

Additional report

Another coverage item about a development that has already been counted. It increases attention, but it does not create another development.

Five-market pilot

Why Canada, China, France, the United Kingdom and the United States?

The selection supports a focused comparison across leading AI ecosystems and English, French and Chinese Google News environments.

What the globe shows

A marker shows the country and language setting used for the Google News search. It does not automatically show where the reported development happened.

What the sample is for

The five markets provide leadership relevance, language variation and different media and policy systems within a manageable pilot scope.

What AIEO does not claim

These are not presented as the definitive top five AI countries. Rankings vary by whether they measure investment, research, infrastructure, adoption or government readiness.

External ranking context

The 2026 Global AI Index ranks the United States 1st, China 2nd, the United Kingdom 3rd, France 6th and Canada 8th. AIEO therefore describes them as five leading AI ecosystems, not as an exact top-five ranking.

Primary relationship lens

Code both sides before naming the relationship

AIEO adapts a two-dimensional human-GenAI configuration framework to news. It classifies how source evidence represents the relation, not objective system performance or AI consciousness.

People enabled + AI side enabledMutualism

Both sides are represented as gaining capacity, value, reach, or effective functioning.

People constrained + AI side enabledAI-benefiting parasitism

The system or operator side is represented as gaining while people lose control, capability, consent, compensation, or protection.

People enabled + AI side constrainedHuman-benefiting parasitism

People gain while the AI system is blocked, degraded, limited, or otherwise constrained.

People constrained + AI side constrainedCompetition or co-constraint

Both sides are represented as losing capacity or effective functioning.

One-sided signal

Only the human side or only the AI side is established. AIEO does not force it into the four complete configurations.

No clear relational signal

Enough evidence is available, but the story does not describe a human-AI relationship.

Insufficient evidence

The stored headline, snippet, or reviewed source material cannot support a defensible relationship judgement.

Human gate: every relationship classification requires an explicit human decision before it is published as reviewed. Model output is a proposal, not the final label.

Two lenses

Attention and reality answer different questions

Coverage Lens

What received attention?

Each newly published AI-relevant article receives one weight. Repetition stays visible because attention and framing matter.

Event Lens

What actually happened?

Each effective resolved event receives one weight, even when many articles or markets cover it.

Directional Amplification Gap = Coverage-Lens human-empowerment index − Event-Lens human-empowerment index

Longitudinal memory

Every return leaves a trace

This occurrence history is what will make replication-delay and media-memory analysis possible after more evidence accumulates.

First coverage

The first observed publication attached to an event.

New coverage of an old event

A new publication instance, but not a new development.

Old article rediscovered

The same stored page reappears in search; no new publication is counted.

Follow-on development

A genuinely new occurrence linked to the same continuing story.

No fixed resurfacing threshold is used. AIEO records the exact time since previous observed coverage when that information is available. A named long-gap category will only be introduced after enough history exists to justify a cut-off empirically.

Revision policy

Corrections accumulate. They do not disappear.

Weekly releases are checked again as the evidence pool grows. Month, quarter and year summaries use the latest accepted event history and state exactly which weekly revisions they include.

  1. Weekly: initial precision-first publication
  2. T+7: recheck the prior week against new evidence
  3. Month close: reconcile the complete month
  4. Quarter close: re-run cross-week and story-family matching
  5. Annual: full-history reconciliation and replication-delay analysis

Secondary human empowerment lens

What changed for people?

Model confidence is diagnostic only. It does not weight the index or replace human audit.

Expanding+ degree / 3

Contracting− degree / 3

AI materially reduces capability, autonomy, rights, protection or control.

Mixed or no direct change0

Effects offset one another, or no supported empowerment mechanism is present.

UnclearExcluded

The evidence is insufficient; the unit does not enter the index denominator.

Limits worth keeping visible

Useful signal, not a census or a final truth

  • Discovery markets describe where searches were run, not the location or empowerment score of an event.
  • The source pool is observed coverage, not every AI article published worldwide.
  • Precision-first resolution prefers temporary false splits over harmful false merges.
  • Same-event recall remains a validation target; later evidence may revise counts.
  • Relationship patterns describe source framings. They do not establish objective benefit, harm, system performance, or legal liability.
  • Complete configurations use only cases where both sides are supported. One-sided and no-clear-signal cases remain visible.
