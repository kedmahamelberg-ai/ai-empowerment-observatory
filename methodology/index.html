Two lenses, two questions

Coverage Lens

Each observed article receives one weight. Repetition is retained because this lens measures attention, repetition and framing.

Event Lens

Each resolved event record receives one weight, regardless of how many articles are currently assigned to it. Event resolution is precision-first: ambiguous possible matches remain separate until reviewed. Event-record counts are therefore not yet claims of perfectly validated unique developments.

What the signal measures

Narrative framing and substantive human empowerment are classified separately. An article can frame AI as an opportunity or threat without documenting a direct change in people’s capability, creativity, autonomy, rights or control.

The empowerment assessment covers four dimensions: operational, creative, agentic and normative. A unit can affect more than one dimension, but its index contribution follows the overall direction and degree supported by the available evidence.

      Scoring

      Expanding: +degree/3 · Contracting: −degree/3 · Mixed: 0 · Non-empowerment: 0 · Unclear: excluded

      The lens index is the arithmetic mean of unit scores × 100. The theoretical range is −100 to +100. The Directional Amplification Gap is Coverage minus Event. These are evidence indices, not sentiment scores and not estimates of AI’s total social benefit or harm.

      Human governance

      The launch baseline used a 20-case stratified human audit: 6 model outputs were accepted and 14 were corrected. That correction share is a diagnostic of the audited cases, not a population accuracy estimate. The corrected launch release is therefore described as a human-audited baseline; later automated releases remain provisional until their audit results are incorporated.

      Model self-confidence is diagnostic only. It is not used to weight the index or, by itself, to decide which routine cases receive human review. Human governance combines stratified sampling with review of higher-risk and methodologically informative cases.

      Limitations

      Current results describe the Observatory’s monitored source set and discovery markets. They should not be interpreted as a census of all AI developments or all global news coverage. Cross-market source coverage, event resolution and classification performance will be expanded and re-audited as the longitudinal series grows.
