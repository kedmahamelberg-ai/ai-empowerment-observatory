From noisy coverage to a people-first picture.

AIEO does not ask you to keep up with every AI headline. It gathers the coverage, groups reports about the same thing, checks what came before, and asks what the news shows for people.

Competition or co-constraint

“AI moves forward” refers to the system, its operator or its wider ecosystem working, spreading or gaining capacity in the source account. It does not suggest that AI is conscious or has human interests.

How source pages become developments

Each AI-related source page published during the weekly period counts once. Pages about the same concrete occurrence are grouped into one development.

Extra pages = source pages minus distinct developments. They show added attention, not an added event.

How first recorded and seen before are decided

A development is first recorded when AIEO does not link it to an accepted development in the disclosed history. A later report about an earlier event adds attention but does not create another event.

Possible links are kept separate while they are checked. Earlier weekly releases can be corrected, and prior versions remain archived.

How several meanings can be stored in one development

The people side and the AI side are considered separately. A development may show people gaining, people losing ground, AI moving forward, AI being limited, or more than one of these at once.

A separate check asks whether benefits differ across groups. This is what allows a story to show both “A mixed picture” and “Not everyone benefits” without forcing a compromise label.

How reliability is checked without making the Observatory manual

The weekly release remains automatic. At selected moments, the owner reviews a full week or a reproducible sample without seeing the existing labels first. Disagreements are measured, approved corrections are added to an owner-judged reference set, and later classification can learn from those decisions.

Historical checks update the week being reviewed and cannot roll the live site back to an older week.

What AIEO does not claim

The discovery markets are a research sample, not a complete census of global AI news. The market where a source was found is not necessarily the place where the story happened.

The Observatory records what the available news supports. A card does not prove a universal outcome, private experience, intention or objective system performance.

What is public—and what remains protected

AIEO publishes the concepts, sources, weekly scope, counts, revisions, limitations and the meaning of every public card. This is enough to understand and challenge the output.

Exact prompts, calibration thresholds, private orchestration code and abuse-resistant operating details are not published. This protects the integrity of the system without hiding how readers should interpret its findings.
