Privacy Notice

How report requests and optional updates are handled

When you request a report, the Observatory may store your name, work email, the report requested, privacy acknowledgement, optional newsletter choice, and submission time. Report requests are used to provide the requested brief. Updates are sent only when you separately opt in.

The report request is processed to provide the requested brief. Optional Observatory updates rely on your separate consent.

You may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent by email.

Consent-controlled analytics measure page use and report downloads. The site does not display visitor metrics publicly.
