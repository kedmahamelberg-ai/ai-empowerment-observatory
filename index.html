Why the two lenses matter

Separate media attention from unique developments

01 · Coverage Lens

What receives attention?

Each article receives one weight. Repeated reporting is retained to measure media volume and framing.

02 · Event Lens

What happened?

Each resolved real-world development receives one weight, regardless of how many articles cover it.

03 · Amplification Gap

How does repetition shift the picture?

Coverage Index minus Event Index reveals whether article volume makes the news environment look more expansionary or more contractionary.
