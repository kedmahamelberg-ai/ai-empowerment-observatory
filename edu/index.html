How should I read this?

AIEO codes the human side and the AI system or operator side independently, then derives the relationship pattern. It classifies how source evidence represents the relation. It does not claim that AI is conscious or that the source proves objective system performance.

Not every AI story contains a two-sided relationship. One-sided signals, no-clear-relationship cases, and insufficient evidence remain visible instead of being forced into the four configurations.
