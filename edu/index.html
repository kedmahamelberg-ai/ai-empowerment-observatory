Six ideas for reading the signal

Use the index as a guide, not a league table

  1. Investment is not empowerment. Capital and infrastructure do not automatically mean more human control.
  2. Capability grows unevenly. AI may strengthen organisations without reaching citizens equally.
  3. Creativity has two sides. New tools can coexist with reduced consent or compensation.
  4. Agency concerns who decides. Productivity gains can accompany transfers of consequential decisions to automated systems.
  5. Rules redistribute power. Transparency, appeal, consultation, and enforcement shape who influences AI.
  6. Read the evidence, not only the score. The signal summarises coded developments; it is not a national ranking.
