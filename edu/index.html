What does “no direct shift identified” mean?

The formal code is non-empowerment. It does not mean “AI has no impact.” It means the available headline/snippet does not establish a direct change in people’s capability, autonomy, rights, protection, participation, or influence.

How should I read the Observatory?
  1. Investment is not empowerment. More money or infrastructure does not automatically mean more human control.
  2. Capability grows unevenly. AI may help organisations without helping everyone equally.
  3. Creativity has two sides. New tools can coexist with less consent or compensation.
  4. Agency asks who decides. Productivity can rise while people lose decision authority.
  5. Rules redistribute power. Transparency, appeal, standards, and enforcement matter.
  6. Read evidence, not only scores. AIEO is a structured signal—not a league table.
