Methodology · Prototype

What the demonstration shows

The observatory will classify AI-related developments across four dimensions: operational, creative, agentic, and normative empowerment. Directional labels will indicate whether human empowerment appears to expand, contract, or remain mixed or unclear.

The three country scores currently shown are fictional interface values. They exist only to test the map, interaction design, and country panel. Source-backed scores and headlines will be added after the coding and validation procedure is established.

Learn · Edu edition

Six ideas for reading the map

  1. Investment is not empowerment. More capital and infrastructure do not automatically mean more human control.
  2. Capability grows unevenly. AI may strengthen organisations without reaching citizens equally.
  3. Creativity has two sides. New creative tools can coexist with reduced consent or compensation for creators.
  4. Agency concerns who decides. Productivity gains can accompany a transfer of consequential decisions to automated systems.
  5. Rules redistribute power. Transparency, consultation, appeal rights, and enforcement shape who influences AI.
  6. Read the evidence, not only the score. The signal summarizes coded developments; it is not a national league table.
