About the initiative

AI Empowerment Observatory — created by Kedma Hamelberg

The AI Empowerment Observatory is a research initiative by Kedma Hamelberg, PhD, an applied AI researcher, builder and educator. It examines what reporting says about AI and human empowerment, with links to the underlying sources and visible limits to the evidence.

One research initiative, two ways to explore

The Observatory presents weekly evidence and its methodology. The Brief provides accessible AI news and research summaries with original source links. Both connect to Kedma Hamelberg’s official website, where you can explore her research, teaching and background.
