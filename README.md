# AI Empowerment Observatory

A public, source-linked research platform that asks what AI developments mean for human capability, creativity, autonomy, rights and control.

## Contribution

The Observatory turns a bounded weekly evidence collection into an inspectable account of who may gain, lose ground or remain uncertain as AI changes work and society. It keeps sources, classification logic, uncertainty and human review visible.

**AI as methodology:** combines computational collection and AI-assisted classification with source records, quality checks and explicit limits on inference.

**AI as a research phenomenon:** analyses AI’s social consequences, including empowerment, dependence, exclusion and institutional change.

**Teaching and impact:** provides a live case for evidence literacy, responsible AI and computational social research, while translating research questions into public-facing material for policymakers, practitioners and citizens.
