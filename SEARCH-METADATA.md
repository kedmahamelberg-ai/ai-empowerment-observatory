# Search identity and image discovery

Kedma Hamelberg’s identity is anchored at https://kedmahamelberg.com/#person. The Observatory and The Brief are distinct organizations and websites connected to her as creator/founder, not alternate profiles of the person. Original research authors and source reporting are not reassigned to Kedma.

Only existing public biographical facts and photos are used. No image licenses or photographer credits are invented. Keep the stable person ID and existing image addresses when updating the site. Personal image metadata is in _data/page_images.json and the photo sitemap; project identity metadata is in config/search-identity.json.

After deployment, submit the sitemaps in Google Search Console and request indexing for the personal homepage, /about/, /photos/, and the two project about pages. Google controls crawling, ranking and image selection; structured data does not guarantee a ranking or a knowledge panel. No paid SEO service is required.
